Description:
`PlanToMeet makes group scheduling fast, simple, and actually usable on iPhone.

Create a poll in seconds, share it in iMessage, and let everyone vote Yes, Maybe, or No without making an account. Friends can respond from the web or jump straight into the App Clip for a native voting experience. When the votes are in, PlanToMeet highlights the best option so you can finalize the plan and add it to calendar.

Features:
- Create scheduling polls with custom date ranges and time slots
- Share polls directly in iMessage
- Let people vote from iPhone, App Clip, or the web
- See responses update in real time
- Highlight the best time automatically
- Finalize plans and add them to calendar
- Clone a finished poll to schedule again faster
- Keep your display name and session synced across devices with iCloud`
