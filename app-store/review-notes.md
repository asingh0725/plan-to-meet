# App Review Information

Fields you still need to enter manually in App Store Connect:

- Contact email
- Contact phone number

Sign-in required:
`No`

Suggested review notes:

`PlanToMeet does not require account creation or login.

Primary review flow:
1. Launch the iOS app and create a scheduling poll.
2. Share the generated poll link.
3. Open the poll link on iPhone to review the App Clip and native voting flow, or open it in Safari to review the web voting flow.
4. Finalize the poll and test Add to Calendar.

Notes for review:
- The app includes a Messages extension for creating and responding to polls in iMessage.
- The App Clip is triggered from poll links under https://plantomeet.app/poll/{pollId}.
- Calendar permission is optional and only requested when the user chooses calendar-related features.
- Push notifications are optional and only requested after explicit user action.
- No paid content, subscriptions, or hidden account-only features are required for review.`
